Zimbabwe: Africa’s Emerging Gemstone Powerhouse

Zimbabwe is rich in a variety of high-quality gemstones — from emeralds and aquamarines to tourmaline and garnet. With increasing government support through the Minerals Marketing Corporation of Zimbabwe (MMCZ) backed initiatives, the country is set to become a key player in the global gemstone trade.
